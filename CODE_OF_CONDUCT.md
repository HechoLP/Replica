# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Replica contributors, maintainers, and users should make participation respectful, safe, and useful. Be considerate of different experience levels, languages, backgrounds, and accessibility needs. Give technical feedback about the work, explain tradeoffs with evidence, and assume good faith while protecting product safety.

Examples of expected behavior include:

- using welcoming and inclusive language;
- disagreeing with ideas without attacking people;
- accepting corrective feedback and acknowledging mistakes;
- protecting private data encountered in reports or reviews;
- keeping security disclosures out of public issues;
- prioritizing user safety over urgency or personal preference.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, publishing private communications or user data, deliberate disruption, impersonation, and retaliation against a good-faith reporter.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, release channels, and other spaces where someone is representing the Replica project.

## Reporting conduct problems

Do not post sensitive conduct reports publicly. Contact the repository owner, [HechoLP](https://github.com/HechoLP), through a private contact method published on that profile. If none is available, open an issue containing only **Private conduct contact requested** and no personal or incident details.

The maintainer will review reports as privately and impartially as practical. People involved in a report should not participate in deciding its outcome when a conflict of interest exists. Knowingly false reports and retaliation are themselves violations; a report that cannot be substantiated is not automatically a false report.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove content, issue a warning, restrict participation, temporarily suspend access, or permanently ban a participant depending on severity, impact, and pattern of behavior. Immediate action may be taken to protect people or private information.
